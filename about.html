About AyurQ

Kerala Ayurveda meets modern luxury wellness — clinical excellence with serene sanctuary.

A Legacy of Restoration

Sustaining Purity: The AyurQ Narrative

Founded on the belief that modern medicine often manages symptoms while neglecting structural balance, AyurQ was born to protect and practice pure, uncompromised Ayurveda. Located in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, we serve as an award-winning center for authentic Kerala therapies and clinical excellence.

Our name represents **"Quality Driven Ayurveda"** — a vow that governs every aspect of our retreat. From importing organically gathered leaves and traditionally cold-pressed oils directly from Kerala, to employing strictly BAMS-trained medical directors, we ensure that clinical protocols conform strictly to Vedic texts.

In our serene retreat, clinical rigour and modern safety protocols merge with a luxurious, peaceful environment. We offer a haven where your body is purified, your mind is stilled, and your innate vitality is fully restored naturally.

Core Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Alignment

Our medical practice rests on three fundamental phases to guarantee true restoration, metabolic longevity, and permanent symptom release.

1. Precise Diagnosis

Through traditional Nadi Pariksha (pulse reading) and physical analysis, our doctors identify the core imbalances (Vikriti) rather than just cataloging physical symptoms.

2. Targeted Therapy

Using classical Kerala clinical methods, we execute physician-guided Panchakarma and oil routines to draw deep metabolic toxins from tissue layers safely.

3. Sustained Lifestyle

We lock in your therapeutic gains by prescribing customized food regimes (Pathya), organic herbs, and simple daily habits tailored to your elemental constitution.

Universal Elemental Principles

The Three Biological Energies

AyurQ restores balance by aligning the three governing principles (Doshas) that shape your biological structure, digestion, and temperamental patterns.

Vata Dosha

Space & Air

Governs all physiological movement, nervous impulses, circulation, and breathing. When balanced, it fuels standard creative expression, lightness, and mental alertness. When disturbed, it triggers joint dryness, chronic insomnia, and nervous anxiety.

Pitta Dosha

Fire & Water

Controls all metabolic digestion, enzymatic transformations, hormonal balance, and temperature regulation. When aligned, it fosters sharp intellect, strong digestion, and drive. When blocked, it sparks inflammatory conditions, acid reflux, and sudden irritability.

Kapha Dosha

Water & Earth

Maintains physical structure, joint lubrication, immunity, and tissue fluid levels. When balanced, it builds bodily strength, emotional patience, and deep immunity. When excessive, it leads to metabolic sluggishness, fluid retention, weight gain, and mental congestion.
