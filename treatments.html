The Five Procedures of Panchakarma

Panchakarma is not a mere massage. It is a highly systemized clinical science comprised of five distinct eliminative procedures to clear structural blockages.

01

Vamana

Therapeutic Emesis: Controlled herbal elimination of metabolic Kapha blockages located in the chest and respiratory channels.

02

Virechana

Purgation: Controlled herbal intestinal cleansing targeting accumulated Pitta toxins in the liver, gallbladder, and blood.

03

Basti

Medicated Enema: The supreme therapy. Introduces specialized oil/decoction feeds to re-align Vata Dosha in the colon.

04

Nasya

Nasal Administration: Herbal drops infused into the nostrils to purge nervous and sensory blockages located above the neck.

05

Raktamokshana

Blood Purification: Traditional targeted therapy (using clinical methods) to resolve chronic dermal conditions and blood impurities.
