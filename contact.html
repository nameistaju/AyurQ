Contact FAQs

Quick answers about our consultation policies, home visits, prior bookings, and vehicle parking amenities.

Our chief medical director and consulting physicians are available from Monday to Saturday, between 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM. On Sundays, the clinic is open only from 9:00 AM - 1:00 PM. Prior booking of appointments is highly recommended to minimize wait times.
Yes, we offer tele-consultations for patients who cannot visit our Visakhapatnam center in person. You can easily schedule an online video consultation by contacting us on WhatsApp (8093526669) or selecting 'Online Consultation' in our appointment booking form. Our medical team will guide you through the process.
Doctor consultations can be arranged selectively at home for elderly, bedridden, or severely arthritic patients within Visakhapatnam city limits. However, traditional Panchakarma therapies must be performed within our clinical rooms under strict sanitization protocols and direct physician supervision. We do not offer therapy setups at home to maintain clinical efficacy and safety.
Yes, AyurQ provides dedicated, secure car and two-wheeler parking spaces directly in front of the center within the Green Park Colony layout. Being located in a quiet, spacious residential lane of Seethammadara, accessing our facilities is entirely stress-free and secure.
As our customized therapy slots fill up fast and require customized herbal decocotions and dedicated therapists, we recommend scheduling your Panchakarma detox or clinical therapies at least 3 to 5 days in advance. You can lock in your dates by submitting our online booking form or speaking directly to our desk on 8093526669.
